# Fact Checklist

Use this file before drafting or when a pleading would otherwise require invented details.

## Required Inputs

Collect or mark placeholders for:

- Document type and user's role.
- Court stage: pre-suit, first instance, second instance, enforcement, preservation, settlement.
- Parties: name, ID number or unified social credit code, address, phone, legal representative if company.
- Legal relationship: contract, loan, lease, labor, tort, marriage/family, consumer, company, other.
- Timeline: agreement date, performance date, breach/injury date, demand date, lawsuit/arbitration date, judgment date.
- Claims: exact act or money requested, amount basis, interest or penalty formula, fee allocation.
- Evidence: name, source, formation time, original carrier, proof purpose.
- Jurisdiction: contract clause, defendant domicile, contract performance place, tort place, property location, or statutory basis.
- Limitation period: date when rights were harmed and any demand, admission, repayment, complaint, mediation, arbitration, or lawsuit.

## Fact Matrix

Before drafting, classify facts as:

| Category | Meaning | Drafting treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed fact | User provided with clear time, actor, and content | Use directly |
| Evidence-backed fact | User provided evidence or document text supports it | Use and connect to evidence |
| User statement | User says it happened but evidence is not clear | Use cautiously or mark evidence gap |
| Missing fact | Needed for document validity or claim calculation | Use bracket placeholder |
| Legal inference | Agent analysis from facts | Keep separate from factual narrative |

## Claim Calculation

For money claims, require:

- Principal amount.
- Start date and end date for interest, penalty, rent, wage, or damages.
- Rate or contractual/statutory basis.
- Whether the amount is fixed, provisional, or calculated to a specific date.
- Whether future interest or delayed performance interest is requested.

If calculation facts are missing, write:

```text
请求判令被告支付[款项性质]人民币[金额待核算]元（具体以证据及庭审查明为准）。
```

## Evidence Mapping

Every important fact should map to at least one evidence item:

- Party identity: ID card, business license, enterprise information.
- Contract relationship: contract, order, quotation, invoice, chat confirmation.
- Performance: delivery note, logistics, acceptance sheet, work record, transfer record.
- Breach or tort: notice, photos, video, repair record, hospital record, police record.
- Loss: invoice, appraisal, payroll, bank statement, repair quotation, rent schedule.
- Demand and negotiation: demand letter, chat record, call recording, complaint record.

## Questions to Ask

Ask only for blocking facts. Prefer a compact list:

```text
还差 4 个信息才能把诉状写实：
1. 被告的身份信息或住所地；
2. 你准备主张的金额及计算依据；
3. 主要证据有哪些；
4. 准备向哪个法院提交，或合同里是否约定管辖。
```

Do not ask for perfect completeness before producing a rough draft if placeholders can safely mark missing fields.
