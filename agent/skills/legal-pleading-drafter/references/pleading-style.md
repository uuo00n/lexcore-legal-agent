# Pleading Style

Use this file to remove AI-like wording from legal pleadings without damaging legal function.

## Core Standard

Write like a careful litigation assistant:

- concrete before abstract;
- evidence before judgment;
- chronology before commentary;
- restraint before emotion;
- claim-by-claim correspondence before broad conclusion.

## Keep These Fixed Legal Forms

Do not treat these as AI-like wording:

- "诉讼请求", "事实与理由", "证据目录", "此致", "具状人", "申请事项", "答辩请求".
- "请求判令", "依法判令", "承担本案诉讼费用".
- "与事实不符", "缺乏证据证明", "没有合同依据或法律依据".
- "暂计至", "以实际清偿之日止", "以法院查明为准".

## Remove or Rewrite AI-like Wording

Avoid:

- Essay transitions: "首先", "其次", "此外", "综上所述", "总而言之", "值得注意的是".
- Empty emphasis: "充分体现", "深刻反映", "彰显", "具有重要意义".
- Customer-service traces: "希望对您有帮助", "建议您咨询专业律师", "以上是我的分析".
- Formulaic contrast: "不是……而是……", "并非……而是……", "一方面……另一方面……".
- Unsupported certainty: "必然胜诉", "法院一定会支持", "毫无疑问", "显然".
- Moral accusation without legal function: "恶意", "极其恶劣", "令人震惊", unless the fact and legal standard require it.

## Preferred Rewrites

| Weak wording | Better pleading wording |
| --- | --- |
| 被告的行为严重侵害了原告的合法权益，性质十分恶劣 | 被告未按约履行付款义务，导致原告至今未收到相应款项 |
| 综上所述，请求法院支持原告诉求 | 据此，原告依法提起诉讼，请求支持前述诉讼请求 |
| 首先，被告存在违约；其次，原告遭受损失 | 被告未按约付款。原告已交付货物并多次催告，但被告仍未支付剩余价款 |
| 这不是普通纠纷，而是严重违约 | 争议焦点在于被告是否应依约支付剩余价款及违约金 |
| 法院必然会支持 | 该请求仍需结合合同约定、履行证据和法院审查结果判断 |

## Sentence Rhythm

- Use shorter sentences for claims.
- Use chronological paragraphs for facts.
- Avoid three-part parallel structures unless listing claims or evidence.
- Prefer one fact per sentence when the fact is important.
- Connect evidence with phrases like "有[证据名称]为证", not broad statements like "证据充分".

## Tone by Document Type

- Complaint: assert claims clearly, but avoid over-arguing.
- Answer: admit, deny, or require proof point by point.
- Appeal: focus on judgment error; avoid re-litigating every fact.
- Preservation: stress necessity and risk, not emotion.
- Enforcement: state effective document and non-performance; avoid repeating trial facts.

## Polish Boundary

When polishing an existing pleading:

- Keep claims, amounts, dates, parties, court names, evidence names, and legal posture unchanged unless the user asks for legal revision.
- If a sentence appears legally wrong, flag it separately instead of silently rewriting it into a new legal position.
- Preserve placeholders and uncertainty labels.
