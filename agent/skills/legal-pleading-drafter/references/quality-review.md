# Quality Review

Use this file before final output.

## Safety Checklist

Confirm:

- No invented party names, ID numbers, addresses, court names, case numbers, dates, amounts, evidence, or statutes.
- Missing required fields remain visible as bracket placeholders.
- Each claim is concrete enough for a court to understand and enforce.
- The facts support every claim.
- Material facts are connected to evidence or marked as needing proof.
- Jurisdiction, limitation period, and amount calculation risks are flagged when uncertain.
- Statutory citations are used only if verified in the current task.
- AI-like wording was removed without deleting necessary legal caution.

## Pleading Completeness

A usable draft should contain:

- Title.
- Parties and litigation role.
- Claims or application matters.
- Facts and reasons.
- Evidence list or evidence mapping when available.
- Court recipient.
- Signature line and date placeholder.
- Supplement list if mandatory fields are missing.

## Risk Flags

Add a short risk note when any item applies:

- The court or jurisdiction basis is unknown.
- The defendant identity or address is incomplete.
- The claim amount lacks calculation basis.
- The limitation period may have expired.
- The evidence only supports part of the claim.
- The requested relief may not match the legal relationship.
- The document type may be wrong for the procedural stage.

## Final Read

Read the final draft as if filing it:

1. Can the clerk identify the parties and document type?
2. Can the judge identify what the user wants?
3. Can the opposing party understand which facts are alleged?
4. Can each important fact be matched to evidence?
5. Does the style sound like a legal document rather than an AI article?

If any answer is "no", revise before presenting the draft.
